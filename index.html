FaithEssaySep 2026

Stewardship is not a spectator sport.

Stewardship is not something we watch other people practice. It is a way of being. For me, it begins with one underlying truth: God exists. When I include Him in the way I live, build, lead, protect, create, and make decisions, everything else begins to find its proper order.

Systems ThinkingNoteAug 2026

What AI actually changed about how I work.

I embraced AI because it became something I had always needed: a personal assistant capable of helping carry the weight of an idea. I used to spend countless hours manually building every individual piece. Now I can work with AI to handle portions of that heavy lifting while I stay focused on the vision, direction, judgment, and purpose. It hasn't replaced how I think. It has expanded what I'm able to build.

Legacy PlanningField NoteJul 2026

Legacy is built, not left.

When I finally understood what mattered most, I made a decision: I would become one of the people in my family responsible for intentionally building legacy. Our children are the future. My responsibility is to take what was given to me, strengthen it, improve it, and place something better into their hands. Legacy isn't something we accidentally leave behind. Legacy is something we build while we're here.

The MissionFig. 05
why any of it matters

Leave people, families, and systems stronger than you found them.

Faith & Family

Faith sits underneath all of it, quietly and on purpose. It's not the loudest thing in the room — it's the thing that determines how I show up in every room. Family is who it's for. Every build, every plan, every creative decision runs through that filter first.

Protection & Stewardship

What you own, you're responsible for. Protect what matters before you chase what's next. I've seen what happens when people build wealth without a plan to protect it. The goal isn't accumulation — it's security for the people who depend on you.

Ownership & Legacy

Freedom follows responsibility. Think in generations, not quarters. The decisions made today either compound into something worth inheriting or they don't. I want everything I build to still be standing — and still be useful — long after I'm done building it.
